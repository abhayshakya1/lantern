# OCBC Outperforms Peers But Underpublishes AI

As of 7 November 2026, OCBC is Singapore's longest-established bank (formed 1932), the second-largest financial group in Southeast Asia with **S$731.7bn of assets**, and the year's best-performing Straits Times Index stock: nine-month 2026 net profit reached a record **S$6.24bn (+10%)**, banking-operations profit hit a record S$1.90bn in 3Q26, loans grew 14%, the NPL ratio fell to 0.8%, and management raised 2026 guidance on 6 November even as the net interest margin compressed to 1.66% ([OCBC 3Q26 press release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/ocbc%203q26%20results%20press%20release.pdf)). Group CEO Tan Teck Long, in post since 1 January 2026 and operating under the eye of the roughly 28%-owning Lee family, has replaced the 2022–2025 "One Group" plan (whose S$3bn incremental-revenue target was beaten in September 2025) with the three-year "Next Frontier" strategy: four shifts (Asia, Tech, Net-Zero, Franchise) with "AI, Digital, Data" (ADD) at the core, a Singapore–Hong Kong twin wealth hub, more than S$1bn a year of technology spend, 600 new relationship managers and only soft financial targets ("stable to improving ROE", cost-to-income in the low-to-mid 40s) ([FY25 CEO deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf)). The structural gaps are equally clear: ROE of 13.5% against DBS's 17.0%, wealth AUM of S$358bn against DBS's S$544bn, a price-to-book above 2x that already discounts the recovery, a 3Q26 group result swung by Great Eastern's bond mark-to-market, and, most relevant to this brief, an AI programme that makes roughly six million decisions a day and runs agentic KYC in production yet publishes no economic-value figure, a research function that issues judgemental house views through a visibly stale web portal, and no quantitative valuation, scenario or forecast-accuracy layer anywhere in the group. That is the opening for an AI-driven forecasting, research-publishing and dashboard platform: pitched as "fit-for-purpose ADD" that industrialises OCBC Group Research's own stated ambition to build "a comprehensive multi-asset research platform", runs on OCBC's on-premise GPU, Cloudera and OpenShift estate, produces the AI inventory, materiality ratings and evaluation evidence that MAS's new Guidelines on AI Risk Management demand by October 2027, and measures its own forecast accuracy and AI value the way DBS already does. The natural sponsors are Kenneth Lai (Global Markets, which since July 2025 owns Group Research, OCBC Securities and A.I. Oscar), Jean Chia and Eli Lee (Bank of Singapore CIO office), Sunny Quek with Chez Anbu (consumer wealth and Wealth Advisory, who distribute through the app and the OCBC WoW avatars), Melvyn Low and Donald MacDonald (Group Strategy and Transformation Office and Group Data Office, the in-house AI builders), Praveen Raina (Group Operations and Technology), with Carina Lee (Group CRO) and Goh Chin Yee (Group CFO) as the risk and value gatekeepers. The user's two papers supply the missing proof points, BharatFinQA's evidence-grounded filing-QA pipeline with pinned-judge evaluation discipline and FinSafeQA-XL's point-in-time, hash-stamped Singapore safe-asset corpus with its grounding-beats-fine-tuning and abstention findings, while the morluto/rea repository turns out to be unrelated to OCBC and contributes only architectural patterns.

## A 1932 merger bank worth S$125bn still answers to its founding family

OCBC is "the longest established Singapore bank, formed in 1932 from the merger of three local banks, the oldest of which was founded in 1912", and it has grown mainly by acquisition ever since ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). Its first Hong Kong branch opened in 1923 and its Xiamen branch in 1925, so the Greater China franchise predates the Singapore merger ([OCBC Group Business](https://www.ocbc.com/group/about-us/group-business)). The modern group was assembled in five moves: Keppel Capital (2001); Bank NISP in Indonesia, where OCBC became majority owner in 2005 after choosing NISP as its partner in 1997 ([OCBC Indonesia history](https://www.ocbc.id/en/profile/sejarah)); ING Asia Private Bank in 2010, which became Bank of Singapore and later absorbed Barclays' Singapore and Hong Kong wealth business (2016) and NAB's private-wealth book (2017) ([Bank of Singapore](https://www.bankofsingapore.com/html/en-sg/who-we-are.html)); Wing Hang Bank in Hong Kong, wholly owned since October 2014 in what Bloomberg describes as a US$5bn deal approved by the Lee family ([Bloomberg via Yahoo, 5 Feb 2026](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/billionaire-owners-seen-posing-challenge-for-new-ocbc-chief-tan-teck-long-032741145.html)); and PT Bank Commonwealth, merged into OCBC Indonesia on 1 September 2024 ([OCBC Indonesia history](https://www.ocbc.id/en/profile/sejarah)). A 12.2% stake in Bank of Ningbo bought in 2006 was raised to the 20% regulatory maximum in 2014 and the alliance was extended for another ten years on 17 September 2026 ([OCBC release, 17 Sep 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-extends-bank-of-ningbo-alliance.page)). In July 2023 the group unified its brand, renaming OCBC Wing Hang as OCBC Bank (Hong Kong) and Banco OCBC Weng Hang as OCBC Bank (Macau), with the China entity renamed OCBC Bank Limited and OCBC NISP adopting the OCBC logo while keeping its legal name ([OCBC release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth)).

### Group structure and the Great Eastern stalemate

OCBC Ltd owns 100% of its banks in Malaysia (including the Islamic OCBC Al-Amin), Hong Kong, Macau and mainland China, 100% of Bank of Singapore and OCBC Securities, 85% of the IDX-listed PT Bank OCBC NISP, 96% (effective) of Lion Global Investors and a **93.72% economic interest in Great Eastern Holdings (GEH)**, all audited by PwC ([AR2025 Note 33.1](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). Lion Global is held 70% by GEH and 30% directly by OCBC ([GEH 2025 Annual Report](https://www.greateasternlife.com/content/dam/corp-site/group/gels-au-corpcomms/english-website/annual-report/great-eastern-holdings-limited-ar-2025.pdf)), and OCBC Bank (Hong Kong) in turn owns OCBC Bank Limited (China), OCBC Bank (Macau) and OCBC Securities Brokerage (Hong Kong) ([OCBC Bank (Hong Kong) Annual Report 2025](https://vpr.hkma.gov.hk/statics/assets/doc/100048/fd_fin/fd_fin_1225_eng.pdf)). The group operates close to 400 branches and representative offices in 19 countries and regions with about 33,000 employees ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [OCBC release, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-holds-industry-first-one-person-company-hackathon-for-33000-employees.page)).

The Great Eastern privatisation, OCBC's fourth attempt in two decades, failed in 2025 and the group has publicly closed the chapter. After a May 2024 offer at S$25.60 took OCBC to 93.72%, GEH's free float fell below 10% and SGX suspended trading from 15 July 2024; on 6 June 2025 OCBC backed a delisting with a S$0.9bn "final" exit offer at S$30.15 for the 6.28% it did not own (0.8x embedded value, 14.3x earnings, EY opining fair and reasonable) ([OCBC release, 6 Jun 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-backs-great-eastern-delisting-proposal.page)). At the 8 July 2025 EGM the delisting resolution drew **63.49% of minority votes against the 75% required**, so the offer lapsed; shareholders instead approved a one-for-one bonus issue in which OCBC elected unlisted Class C non-voting shares, cutting its voting stake to 88.2% while preserving the 93.72% economic interest, and GEH resumed trading on 21 August 2025 ([OCBC statement, 8 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-statement-on-the-approval-of-great-eastern-holdings-resolutions-b-and-c.page); [Dr Wealth](https://drwealth.com/great-eastern-trading-resumes/)). OCBC has said it has "no intention to launch another offer in the foreseeable future" ([OCBC clarification, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/clarification-on-media-report-regarding-ocbcs-future-offer-for-great-eastern-shares.page)), Tan Teck Long called it "a chapter behind us" at the FY2025 briefing ([The Edge via Yahoo, 25 Feb 2026](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/great-eastern-chapter-behind-ocbc-201336496.html)), and the April 2026 AGM confirmed OCBC "is not proactively looking to further increase its shareholding" ([AGM 2026 responses](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2026/OCBC%20AGM%20responses%20to%20substantial%20and%20relevant%20questions%20from%20shareholders%20and%20SIAS%2010%20April%202026.pdf)). Bloomberg reports the bid fell "at least S$230 million short of minority holders' demands" and that distant Lee relatives who hold GEH shares resisted it ([Bloomberg via Yahoo](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/billionaire-owners-seen-posing-challenge-for-new-ocbc-chief-tan-teck-long-032741145.html)). GEH should therefore be treated as a permanently listed, 93.72%-owned subsidiary whose minority (12% of votes) still matters.

### Ownership: a public float of 72% but a family that must still approve big decisions

OCBC trades on the SGX Main Board as O39 (ISIN SG1S04926220) with 4,501,436,229 issued shares, 131,650 shareholders and about 72.3% public float at 25 February 2026 ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). At the 6 November 2026 close of **S$27.73** the market capitalisation was about **S$124.7bn**, down from S$130.5bn in mid-October after the shares peaked at S$32.57 ([Yahoo Finance](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/quote/O39.SI/)); the stock was still up 46.8% year-to-date on 23 October and was the top STI performer ([OCBC release, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-strongest-bank-in-singapore-and-asia-pacific.page)). The register is dominated by Lee-family vehicles: Selat (Pte) Limited holds 14.44% including Herald Investment, Lee Foundation 5.13% including Lee Pineapple and Peninsula Plantations, and Singapore Investments, Lee Rubber, Kallang Development and Kew Estate appear on the top-20 list, which together with Citibank Nominees (16.23%) and the other nominee lines accounts for 72.21% of the shares ([AR2025 shareholding statistics](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). Bloomberg puts the family's aggregate at about **28%**, roughly half of its US$38bn fortune, paying more than US$1bn a year in dividends; it notes that "key decisions still need the family's approval", that the family derailed a ~S$2bn headquarters renovation and a sweetened Great Eastern bid, and that Prof Lee Tih Shih, the sole family director since 2015, chairs the board's Executive Committee (a seat held by the chairman at DBS and UOB) and helped recruit Tan Teck Long from DBS ([Bloomberg via Yahoo, 5 Feb 2026](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/billionaire-owners-seen-posing-challenge-for-new-ocbc-chief-tan-teck-long-032741145.html)). The practical implication for any vendor is that capital-intensive proposals meet a conservative shareholder whose influence runs through the Exco, not just the AGM.

### Bank of Singapore: past its US$145bn target, aiming for Asia's top five

Bank of Singapore, the wholly owned open-architecture private bank, "surpassed our target of US$145 billion in assets under management (AUM) set in 2023, growing our AUM by over 20% in 2025", with revenue also up more than 20%, UHNW AUM up 25% and financial-intermediary AUM up more than 30% ([AR2025, Private Banking review](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). It runs about 500 relationship managers (400 in 2023), hubs in Singapore, Hong Kong and Dubai (where it ranks third behind Julius Baer and UBS and targets 20% of AUM by 2027), plus BOS Wealth Management Europe in Luxembourg and BOS Wealth Management Malaysia ([Private Banker International](https://www.privatebankerinternational.com/news/bank-of-singapore-technology-hiring/)). Its stated ambition is to be "the top Asian private bank, and among the top five private banks in Asia, in the next three to five years", it targets 30% growth in UHNW AUM by 2028, and in June 2026 it reorganised into ASEAN, Greater China and Middle East/Global South Asia market groups while launching a "Family Office Catalyst" vehicle ([Bank of Singapore release, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.bankofsingapore.com/media-releases/2026/bank-of-singapore-veteran-lim-leong-guan-to-helm-dubai-business-amidst-ultra-high-net-worth-push.html)). CEO Jason Moo noted that its July 2025 strategic asset allocation framework, "developed by our Chief Investment Office after a year-long study and stress testing of 120,000 portfolios", made it "the first Asian private bank to use the robust optimisation technique" ([finews.asia, 15 Oct 2025](https://www.finews.asia/finance/44173-bank-of-singapore-private-banking-ocbc-uhnw-eam-aum-jason-moo)). Its AI stack is already substantial: HOLMES AI gives RMs tailored investment insights, the agentic Source of Wealth Assistant (June 2025, built with the Group Data Office) cut report preparation "from 10 days to just one hour", HELIOS (July 2026) front-loads due diligence so accounts open in 15 business days against an industry median of about six weeks, and an unnamed external "AI wealth platform" serves financial intermediaries ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [OCBC release, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-harnesses-agentic-ai-to-quicken-onboarding-customers)). A vendor lands better by augmenting CIO content distribution and portfolio analytics than by replacing these tools.

### Great Eastern: record profit, 16 million policyholders, volatile marks

Founded in 1908, Great Eastern has more than S$123bn of assets and more than 16 million policyholders (11.7 million through government schemes), is rated AA by Fitch and AA- by S&P, and sells through a tied agency force of roughly 25,000, bancassurance, digital partners and Great Eastern Financial Advisers in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brunei ([GEH 9M26 release](https://www.greateasternlife.com/sg/en/about-us/media-centre/media-releases/great-eastern-sustains-strong-new-business-momentum-9m26.html)). FY2025 profit attributable rose 21% to a record **S$1,207m** and new business embedded value 19% to S$740m even as total weighted new sales fell 15% to S$1,535m on a deliberate retreat from single-premium products; group embedded value reached S$20.1bn ([GEH FY25 summary](https://www.greateasternlife.com/content/dam/corp-site/great-eastern/sg/gels-au-group-mgmt/investor-relations/2025-fy25-financial-summary%20(SGX).pdf)). ### Seven quarters: a trough in 4Q25, a record in 2Q26, a GEH-driven dip in 3Q26

Quarterly net profit bottomed at S$1.75bn in 4Q25 (impaired allowances of S$236m "largely for two corporate real estate accounts" and a Malaysian medical-insurance adjustment at GEH), set a record S$2.22bn in 2Q26, and came in at **S$2.04bn in 3Q26** (+3% year on year, −8% quarter on quarter), beating the S$2.00–2.01bn consensus ([FY25 media release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20media%20release%20financial%20highlights.pdf); [Beansprout, 6 Nov 2026](https://growbeansprout.com/ocbc-3q26-profit-dividend)). NIM compressed every quarter from 2.04% (1Q25) to 1.66% (3Q26) as average 3-month SORA fell from 2.33% to 1.16%, but the 3Q26 bridge shows the inflection: loan yields +3bps, treasury yields +3bps, funding costs −11bps, mix +2bps, and management stated that "higher SORA rates were a net positive to NII as asset yields rose faster than funding costs" while "the benefits from higher US rates would be reflected in the coming quarters as assets reprice" ([3Q26 press release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/ocbc%203q26%20results%20press%20release.pdf)). Net interest income of S$2.31bn in 3Q26 was the highest in six quarters, loans grew 14% year on year to S$372bn "led by high-growth sectors including TMT, digital infrastructure, transportation and power & utilities, and real estate in the developed markets", deposits grew 12% to S$461bn, CASA slipped to 48.4%, credit costs were just 6bps and NPA coverage rose to 168% ([3Q26 press release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/ocbc%203q26%20results%20press%20release.pdf)). Banking Operations profit has risen for three straight quarters to a record S$1.90bn (+16% year on year), so the 3Q26 group dip is entirely Great Eastern's mark-to-market, which is why management now reports the two separately ([3Q26 results highlights](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/OCBC%203Q26%20Results%20Highlights.pdf)).

Guidance was upgraded at every 2026 call. In February Tan guided "stable to growing" total income, a slight-to-moderate NII decline, mid-single-digit loan growth, 20–25bps credit costs and a low-to-mid 40% cost-to-income ratio assuming 3-month SORA of about 1.4% and Fed funds of about 3.5%; by August loan growth was "high-single-digit to low-double-digit" and the ~S$800m balance of the S$2.5bn capital return would, if not bought back, be paid as a special dividend of roughly 18 cents with the FY2026 final; on 6 November loan growth became "low-double-digit", total income "at least mid-single digit", credit costs 15–20bps, and the rate assumption flipped to "one additional Fed Fund Rate hike in 4Q26" with 3-month SORA about 1.2% and HIBOR about 2.9% ([FY25 CEO deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf); [Investing.com 2Q26 call transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972); [3Q26 CEO deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/OCBC%203Q26%20CEO%20Presentation.pdf)). ### Capital returns: 60% payouts, S$2.5bn programme, 14% CET1 floor

OCBC's policy is a 50% ordinary payout of core net profit paid half-yearly with a minimum "A" credit-rating target; on top of that the February 2025 plan returns S$2.5bn over two years via special dividends equal to 10% of FY2024 and FY2025 profit plus buybacks, lifting total payouts to 60% in both years ([FY2024 release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-group-full-year-2024-net-profit-grew-8-percent-to-a-record-7point59-billion.page); [AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)). S$1.7bn had been returned by February 2026 (S$0.7bn specials each year plus cancelled buybacks), the FY2025 dividend of 99 cents cost S$4.45bn, the 1H26 interim rose 15% to 47 cents (S$2.11bn), and Tan said in August that OCBC is "very comfortable operating at CET1 CAR at around 14%" fully phased-in with no equity raise anticipated ([Chairman's Reflections](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/chairmans-reflections.page); [1H26 media release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/ocbc%201h26%20media%20release%20financial%20highlights.pdf); [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972)). Second, the re-rating from a historical average of about 1.18x book to 2.03x has outrun the ROE improvement, which is the core of the bear case: Citi downgraded to Sell (S$27.50) on 8 October and Macquarie to Hold (S$30.00) on 14 October citing valuation and NIM pressure, JPMorgan holds Sell at S$25.00, while UOB Kay Hian (S$35.70), Maybank (S$37.23) and Jefferies (Hold, S$35.00) sit in a bull cluster; Phillip's Accumulate uses a Gordon-growth 2.31x FY26 book at 14.5% ROE ([Investing.com consensus](https://www.investing.com/equities/oversea-chinese-banking-corp.-consensus-estimates); [POEMS Phillip, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.poems.com.sg/stock-research/OCBS.SG/); [Beansprout, 6 Nov 2026](https://growbeansprout.com/ocbc-3q26-profit-dividend)). Third, contrary to a common assumption, OCBC's forward yield (about 3.6%) is now the lowest of the three because its price rose fastest; dividend support rests on the FY2026 final plus the residual special ([Beansprout compare page](https://growbeansprout.com/quote/D05.SI/compare)). OCBC is the second-largest STI constituent at 18.3% behind DBS at 29.2%, so the three banks are 56.7% of the SPDR STI ETF ([SPDR STI ETF holdings, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.ssga.com/sg/en_gb/institutional/etfs/funds/spdr-straits-times-index-etf-es3)). Year-end closes trace the run: S$12.18 (2022), S$13.00 (2023), S$16.69 (2024), S$19.76 (2025), a monthly peak of S$31.99 at end-September 2026 and S$27.73 on 6 November ([Yahoo Finance chart data](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/O39.SI?range=5y&interval=1d)).

## The S$3bn One Group target gave way to a Next Frontier with few hard numbers

Helen Wong's 2022 refresh was an eight-pillar, three-year plan: four "Drive Growth" priorities (capture rising Asian wealth through Singapore–Hong Kong hubs, support ASEAN–Greater China trade and investment flows, unlock value from the New Economy, drive the low-carbon transition) and four "Reinforce Strengths" enablers (a "One Group" integrated customer approach, transformation and digitalisation, people and culture, capital and risk management) ([OCBC ASEAN–Greater China Update, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf)). In July 2023 it was sharpened into an ASEAN–Greater China thesis with a headline target of **S$3bn of cumulative incremental revenue over 2023–2025 "on top of current growth trajectory"**, four goals (top-two financial institution in ASEAN by assets, structurally stronger income generation, better cost-income, focused capital deployment) and a set of sub-targets: more than S$50m invested in Greater China transaction banking, 500 regional cash-management mandates, doubled investment-banking revenue, 26,000 new Hong Kong SME customers, a 50% rise in Greater China franchise revenue in ASEAN by 2025, Bank of Singapore at US$145bn AUM and 500 RMs by end-2025, and doubled Greater China premier RMs and AUM ([OCBC release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth); [2023 deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf)). Progress was reported as S$500m of incremental revenue in 2023, the Greater China-in-ASEAN revenue goal met "ahead of time for the second consecutive year" in 2024 with a near-30% rise in new-to-bank Chinese companies entering ASEAN, and then: "In September 2025, we surpassed ahead of time our target to deliver $3 billion in incremental revenue above the 2023 growth trajectory" ([AR2024](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2024/ocbc-annual-report-2024.pdf); [AR2025, The Power of One Group](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/the-power-of-one-group.page)). OCBC never published the annual attribution beyond the 2023 figure, so an outside analyst cannot reconstruct how the S$3bn was counted; the FY2023–2025 scorecard it did publish shows banking pre-tax profit flat (S$7.5bn to S$7.7bn, a 1% CAGR), group wealth income up at a 14% CAGR to S$5.6bn, wealth AUM up 14% a year to S$343bn and GEH's contribution up 33% a year ([AR2025, The Power of One Group](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/the-power-of-one-group.page)). The "top two in ASEAN" goal was declared met by the chairman's statement that the S$676bn combined asset base makes OCBC "the second largest financial services group in Southeast Asia" ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf)).

### The Next Frontier (25 February 2026): four shifts, ADD at the core, soft targets

Tan Teck Long unveiled "The Next Frontier" with the FY2025 results rather than at an investor day, and restated it at the 16 April 2026 AGM. It names five megatrends (geopolitical tension, demographics, rising Asian wealth, AI and digitalisation, sustainability) and four strategic shifts with "ADD at our core": an **Asia Shift** ("capture ASEAN–Greater China flows", a "One-ASEAN" proposition, "rising Asian wealth flows through our SG-HK twin wealth hubs"); a **Tech Shift** ("Customer Centric ADD Strategy – Right Customer, Right Product, Right Time" and "intensify focus and coverage on technology, including digital infrastructure and tech supply chain"); a **Net-Zero Shift** (green sectors, SME transition); and a **Franchise Shift** (Singapore: "Whole-of-Wealth" across banking, wealth and insurance plus a "Silver proposition for the super-aged society"; Hong Kong: grow the affluent segment and "scale up Global Markets"; Malaysia: affluent growth via the Great Eastern Malaysia ecosystem and JS-SEZ flows; Indonesia: "Grow OCBC Private Bank") ([FY25 CEO deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf); [AGM 2026 presentation](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2026/ocbc%20management%20presentation%20at%201pm%20prior%20to%202026%20agm%20on%2016%20april%202026.pdf)). The financial framing is deliberately soft: "Expect stable to improving ROE, driven by a stronger focus on higher-returning businesses" and "Maintain cost discipline with CIR at low to mid 40%"; asked in August for an aspirational ROE, Tan said "I'm not quite ready to share because there are so many factors involved" ([Investing.com transcript, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972)). Morgan Stanley's Nick Lord said the strategy "makes sense" but offers no concrete ROE target and no special-dividend guidance beyond 2026 ([Bloomberg via Yahoo, Feb 2026](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/ocbc-profit-climbs-on-wealth-as-new-ceo-tan-shares-strategy-041146914.html)). The AGM added texture: the strategy is three years long, "Data is the foundation, Digital enables customer and employee journeys, and AI is added when it becomes fit for purpose and cost-effective", "Indonesia stays", first-order Middle East exposure is about 2–3% of lending, and a S$5bn office redevelopment was deferred ([The Fifth Person, 20 Apr 2026](https://fifthperson.com/2026-ocbc-agm/)). The quantified commitments are inputs rather than outputs: "more than SGD 1 billion annually in technology over the next few years", 600 additional relationship managers over three years, a Wealth Management Committee chaired by Tan with the heads of OCBC, Bank of Singapore and Great Eastern, and "double-digit [AUM] growth over the next few years" ([The Asian Banker, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/how-ocbc-is-executing-its-next-frontier-strategy-through-ai-digital-and-data); [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972)). A strategy that specifies inputs but not outputs, and guidance revised three times in a year, leaves room for a proposal framed around measuring and forecasting the outcome of those inputs.

### Wealth, sustainability, M&A and people: the concrete 2026 commitments

Wealth is the engine of both strategies. Sunny Quek "reiterated the goal of doubling consumer banking wealth income by 2029, one year earlier than previously set"; OCBC will add 30–50 relationship managers in Hong Kong in 2026 and aims to be a top-10 lender there by 2030 after 1Q26 Hong Kong net profit rose 88% and wealth income 50%; Bank of Singapore targets 30% UHNW AUM growth by 2028 with Dubai as a third hub and a rebuilt family-office team under Paul Chua ([The Asian Banker, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/how-ocbc-is-executing-its-next-frontier-strategy-through-ai-digital-and-data); [Reuters via Yahoo, 15 May 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/singapores-ocbc-add-30-50-052220483.html); [WealthBriefingAsia, 29 Apr 2026](https://www.wealthbriefingasia.com/article.php/Bank-Of-Singapore-Pushes-Further-Into-Family-Office,-UHNW-Sector)). On sustainability, the S$50bn-by-2025 target was beaten in September 2023 at S$52bn, after which Helen Wong said "whatever target you set becomes just a number to be beaten"; commitments reached S$80bn at end-2025 and the live targets are now sectoral (net-zero financed emissions by 2050 with 2030 targets for power, oil and gas, real estate, steel, aviation and shipping, covering close to 40% of corporate lending), an SME target (12,000 SMEs and S$25bn of SME commitments by 2028) and up to S$17bn of UK financing by 2030; OCBC reports MSCI AA and FTSE4Good membership but no Sustainalytics or CDP score ([The Edge via Yahoo, 3 Dec 2023](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/ocbc-sustainable-finance-loan-book-064600756.html); [OCBC release, 16 May 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unveils-decarbonisation-targets-for-six-sectors-to-achieve-net-zero-financed-emissions-by-2050.page); [OCBC release, 7 Apr 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-aims-to-support-12000-smes-across-its-core-markets); [Sustainability Report 2025 via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/oversea-chinese-banking-ocbc-sustainability-report-2025-english--ce7e5eddd98ff727)). On M&A, Tan is explicit: "We are very disciplined with acquisition ... my personal preference is portfolio relating to wealth, like the HSBC wealth acquisition ... we are actually quite risk averse"; the pattern is bolt-on retail and wealth books in Indonesia (PT Bank Commonwealth, completed May 2024; HSBC Indonesia, closing 2Q27) and no disposals ([Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972); [OCBC release, May 2024](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2024/ocbc-deepens-indonesia-presences-with-pt-bank-commonwealth-acquisition.page)). Headcount was 33,323 at end-2025 (−1%); about three in five employees have taken AI, digital or data training in three years under an "AI for All / AI for Practitioners / AI for Leaders" framework with 17-week analytics and 22-week "Data Science and Agentic AI" tracks, and the annual report's stance is that "many of today's roles may evolve or even disappear as automation accelerates ... our approach is grounded in redesigning work around people" ([AR2025](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [Mothership citing Business Times, 24 Apr 2026](https://mothership.sg/2026/04/dbs-ocbc-uob-job-cuts/)). The vocabulary a pitch should mirror is therefore fixed: "ADD", "Right Customer, Right Product, Right Time", "fit-for-purpose", "human-in-the-loop", "Whole-of-Wealth", "twin hubs", "One Group", "Asia/Tech/Net-Zero/Franchise Shift", "silver proposition", "China plus n".

## OCBC spends over S$1bn a year on technology but cannot say what AI returns

OCBC has been building AI in-house since it launched "AI Lab@TOV" on 14 March 2018 with an initial S$10m, three data scientists and an "AI-first" ambition; that unit became the Group Data Office under Donald MacDonald in June 2022, and by the 2024 annual report "about six million decisions in the Bank are made by AI each day" across "over 300 AI use cases" ([OCBC release, 2018](https://ocbc.com/group/media/release/2018/ocbc-launches-ai-lab); [OCBC release, 8 Jun 2022](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2022/ocbc-new-group-data-head.page); [AR2024, Creating Value Through AI](https://ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/creating-value-through-ai.page)). The generative wave started with **OCBC GPT**, built in OCBC's private Azure environment on OpenAI models and delivered through Microsoft Teams to about 30,000 staff in November 2023 after a six-month trial in which investment-research, product and marketing staff reported finishing tasks about 50% faster; alongside it sit Wingman (a coding assistant giving developers 20–30% uplift, built in-house in under two weeks), Buddy (a knowledge assistant over 150,000 pages) and Document AI ([OCBC release, 24 Oct 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-is-first-singapore-bank-to-roll-out-generative-ai-chatbot-to-all-employees-globally.page); [Microsoft, 8 Nov 2023](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/ocbcs-new-generative-ai-chatbot-is-boosting-the-banks-productivity-across-departments-and-locations/)). MacDonald's design choices are the ones a vendor must respect: about 80% of the work is still traditional machine learning, generative AI runs "largely on-premise using open-source tools on its large GPU infrastructure" at "effectively zero" incremental cost, one contact-centre use case would have cost about $3m in public-cloud API calls, roughly 350 systems feed a Cloudera data platform adopted in 2015, "we have 30 plus Gen AI applications running in production", HOLMES processes 200,000 pages of research a day into RM talking points, scam screening runs in under 15 milliseconds and most use cases are built in under a month when the data is ready ([The Asian Banker, Jan 2024](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/ocbcs-donald-macdonald-genai-integration-increased-efficiency); [CMOtech Asia, 19 Aug 2025](https://cmotech.asia/story/exclusive-ocbc-s-20-year-data-ai-innovation-journey)). The 2024 report also set 2027 targets with no stated baseline: 75% of customer-service requests AI-assisted, 100% of roles augmented, 20% higher staff productivity and "2x revenue contributed by AI" ([AR2024](https://ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/creating-value-through-ai.page)).

### 2026: the narrative moved from internal productivity to customer-facing wealth AI

The 2025 annual report highlights agentic AI in KYC (tasks that took up to ten days now take about an hour, with a human in the loop), an AI Copilot for corporate RMs that generates ESG, financial and industry analysis and cuts credit-assessment preparation by up to 30%, agentic code assistance worth up to 30% engineer productivity, 20–30% less coding and testing effort, voice AI for reminder calls and "close to S$80 million in cost avoidance from technology modernisation" ([AR2025, AI/Digital/Data](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/driving-customer-centricity-through-ai-digital-and-data.page); [AR2025, Operations and Technology](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/year-in-review/operations-and-technology.page)). Three 2026 launches define the current posture. On 15 April OCBC rolled out six months of generative-AI skills training to about 900 Singapore wealth advisers, with LLM-generated client scenarios built on anonymised behavioural data; trained advisers booked double the weekly appointments of untrained peers and revenue rose 50% over three months ([OCBC release, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-launches-generative-ai-powered-skills-training-for-all-wealth-advisors.page)). On 1 July it unveiled **OCBC WoW**, billed as "the first AI-native bank mobile app in South-East Asia", with avatars "Wendy" and "Wayne" that deliver "personalised OCBC Group insights and house views" on a four-layer architecture (data ingestion, deterministic guardrails, AI agents, output modules), in an invitation-only beta for staff and Premier Private Client customers with at least S$1.5m, English first and Mandarin, Bahasa Melayu and Bahasa Indonesia to follow; Fortune reported the avatars are "trained on proprietary data from the bank's research team" ([OCBC release, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-unveils-avatar-banking); [Fortune, 1 Jul 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/01/ocbc-avatar-banking-ai-wendy-wayne-ceo-tan-teck-long/)). On 29 July Bank of Singapore launched HELIOS, an agentic due-diligence platform that surfaces compliance-qualified leads to RMs and targets 15-business-day account opening, with Loretta Yuen calling it "a paradigm shift for compliance where we are not just enabling business, but originating opportunities" ([OCBC release, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-harnesses-agentic-ai-to-quicken-onboarding-customers)). The culture is employee-led experimentation: a 22 October 2026 "One-Person Company" hackathon for all 33,000 staff drew thousands of AI-agent submissions, about three-quarters from non-technology functions, with more than 400 prototypes screened by internally built agents and finalists refined at the SIT x NVIDIA AI Technology Centre ([OCBC release, 26 Oct 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-holds-industry-first-one-person-company-hackathon-for-33000-employees.page)). OCBC Malaysia alone spends more than RM10m a year on AI-focused people development across about 480 programmes ([The Star, 12 Sep 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2025/09/12/ocbc-malaysia-invests-over-rm10mil-a-year-in-ai-driven-people-development)).

What OCBC does not have is a way to say what any of this earns. Tan's position is that "AI is just a tool" to be embedded in process redesign, that OCBC "expects to spend over one billion dollars a year on technology for the next three years" without separating AI from the total, and that "you have to spend a lot of energy to quantify the benefits of AI ... the energy we spend trying to do so may be equal to the amount of time we take to roll out new AI initiatives"; in August he added "it's quite difficult to identify exactly how much is due to AI" and "when it comes to AI, actually, we are very cost conscious" ([The Asian Banker, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/how-ocbc-is-executing-its-next-frontier-strategy-through-ai-digital-and-data); [Fortune, 1 Jul 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/07/01/ocbc-avatar-banking-ai-wendy-wayne-ceo-tan-teck-long/); [Investing.com transcript, 7 Aug 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-ocbc-posts-record-q2-2026-profit-as-shares-near-high-93CH-4845972)). Critics have noticed: The Asian Banker's October 2026 assessment says OCBC's AI narrative lacks spending data, outcome metrics and peer benchmarking, with benefits "framed as possibilities rather than demonstrated results", Fintech News calls the "300+" use-case count "older data" against DBS's 430+ and S$1bn of economic value, and Bloomberg's coverage of the tech budget warns that rising costs "could squeeze the cost-to-income ratio" under scrutiny from long-term shareholders ([The Asian Banker, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/how-can-ocbc-sustain-its-financial-strength-amid-structural-change); [Fintech News Singapore, 14 Oct 2026](https://fintechnews.sg/137393/ai/how-dbs-ocbc-and-uob-are-betting-on-ai-in-2026/); [The Next Web, 1 Jul 2026](https://thenextweb.com/news/ocbc-annual-tech-spending-771-million-ai)). If the S$1bn is realised it is roughly 17% of FY2025's S$5.88bn opex; 1H26 opex already grew 10% and 3Q26's cost-to-income was 40.8%, so the cost line is absorbing the spend and the bar for any vendor to show attributable revenue or cost-out is rising ([OCBC 1H26 release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-group-first-half-2026-net-profit-grew-13percent); [3Q26 release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-group-third-quarter-2026-results)).

### Infrastructure, leadership and the scam-shaped risk culture

OCBC's stated posture is hybrid cloud "so that we don't have any dependency with any particular provider": phase one moved internet banking, mobile and contact-centre applications, phase two targets payments and loans, and the Enterprise Data Science Platform runs on Red Hat OpenShift over Cloudera and Hadoop; AI Lab job postings prefer an "on-premise distributed framework" with Hadoop, Trino and an Elastic vector database, while a data-platform posting lists AWS S3, Glue, Athena, EMR and SageMaker across a hybrid estate ([Frontier Enterprise, Jan 2023](https://www.frontier-enterprise.com/ocbc-bank-bets-on-open-source-ai-to-stay-ahead-of-the-game/); [Built In Singapore](https://builtinsingapore.com/job/data-scientist-ai-lab/8138191)). No Google Cloud relationship exists beyond 2018 voice banking, Microsoft appears as the Azure OpenAI and Teams host, and partners are large regulated counterparties (J.P. Morgan Kinexys, Citi, Swift, Ant International, Visa, OneConnect) rather than startups; OCBC has no venture arm ([Microsoft, Nov 2023](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/ocbcs-new-generative-ai-chatbot-is-boosting-the-banks-productivity-across-departments-and-locations/)). Multi-year commitments include the S$500m OCBC Punggol hub for up to 4,000 mostly technology staff due in 1Q27 with an SIT lab, HK$1.5bn for Greater China technology by 2026 including a near-HK$1bn modernisation of the Hong Kong platform, engineering hubs in Kuala Lumpur, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Jakarta, a S$240m regional data centre, a quantum roadmap with QKD over Singtel's network and NUS, NTU and SMU research projects, and SOC 2 Type II plus the highest-tier Cyber Trust Mark ([OCBC release, 18 Sep 2024](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2024/ocbc-to-invest-500-million-in-punggol-digital-district.page); [OCBC release, 17 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-deepens-research-in-quantum-technology-with-nus-ntu-smu); [GovWare](https://www.govware.sg/speakers/david-ng-v2sd)). Decision rights are split three ways: Praveen Raina's Group Operations and Technology owns infrastructure, architecture and cyber (David Ng's Technology Information Security Office; Thomas Kok's ISDRM reports into Risk), MacDonald's Group Data Office builds models under Melvyn Low's Strategy and Transformation Office, and business sponsorship comes from Quek, Moo, Lai and Ling; the "Group CISO" title was held concurrently by CRO Noel D'Cruz until his retirement and its current holder is unconfirmed ([OCBC 2024 management committee](https://ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2024/further-info-on-management-committee.pdf)).

The risk culture was shaped by the December 2021 SMS-spoofing scam: 790 customers lost S$13.7m, OCBC reimbursed all of it as a one-off goodwill gesture, and on 26 May 2022 MAS imposed a 1.3x operational-risk RWA multiplier worth about **S$330m of additional capital** for deficient incident response ([MAS release, 26 May 2022](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2022/mas-imposes-additional-capital-requirement-on-ocbc-bank-for-deficiencies-in-response-to-spoofed-sms-phishing-scams); [OCBC statement](https://ocbc.com/group/media/release/2022/media-statement-mas-response.page)). The remedies became products: a kill switch (February 2022), 12-hour cooling-off periods, Android anti-malware blocking that saved over S$2m in its first month, Money Lock (over S$3.3bn locked within two months), in-app calls and AI payment screening ([OCBC release, 16 Feb 2022](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2022/ocbc-rolls-out-emergency-kill-switch.page); [OCBC release, 25 Jan 2024](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2024/ocbc-extends-money-lock-anti-scam-feature-to-time-deposits.page)). Outages recurred without disclosed root causes on 28 August 2023, 18 December 2025 and 23 October 2026 (a Friday-afternoon login and PayNow disruption with 767 Downdetector reports at its peak that OCBC called "intermittent slowness"), and a May 2026 dark-web claim of an OCBC Malaysia breach was found to be fabricated ([The Star, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/10/23/ocbc-customers-report-difficulties-accessing-mobile-app-and-internet-banking); [Lowyat.NET, May 2026](https://www.lowyat.net/2026/391717/ocbc-and-uob-malaysia-allegedly-hit-by-data-breach-claims/)). Recognition is steady but second-tier: Euromoney's best digital bank in Singapore in 2024, Asian Banker awards for GenAI productivity (2024) and AI personalisation and process automation (2025), and two TAB Global AI Excellence Awards in May 2026 (the RM Credit Copilot and AI infrastructure), against DBS's global sweep ([Euromoney, 2024](https://www.euromoney.com/article/2di0dggczcqyyt26b40sh/awards/awards-for-excellence/awards-for-excellence-best-digital-bank-in-singapore-ocbc); [The Asian Banker, 14 May 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/ocbc-pudao-credit-kasikornbank-bank-mandiri-and-atmaal-recognised-for-excellence-in-ai-deployment-across-asia-pacific)). Hiring in 2025–2026 targets AI Lab data scientists building "agentic systems", RAG and recommenders, hybrid AWS platform engineers, digital-asset custody analysts and cyber roles; no quantitative-research or forecasting role was found, which is consistent with a bank that builds distribution and operations AI but not a forecasting layer ([Built In Singapore](https://builtinsingapore.com/job/vice-president-business-analyst-digital-assets-digital-custody/10195886)).

## Capital is ample; the binding constraints are margin, Greater China property and MAS's rulebook

OCBC is one of seven MAS-designated domestic systemically important banks and must hold CET1, Tier 1 and total capital of at least 6.5%, 8.0% and 10.0% of risk-weighted assets plus a 2.5-point capital conservation buffer, i.e. 9.0%, 10.5% and 12.5% all-in, with a countercyclical buffer currently at zero ([AR2025, Capital Management](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [MAS D-SIB framework, 2015](https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/data/pdfdoc/20150507006/press_release_-_mas_publishes_framework_for_domestic_systemically_important_banks_in_singapore.pdf)). At 30 September 2026 it reported CET1 of 15.5% on the transitional final-Basel-III basis and **14.0% fully phased-in** (its own operating target), Tier 1 of 16.4%, total capital of 18.3%, a 6.5% leverage ratio, all-currency LCR of 130%, SGD LCR of 211% and NSFR of 111%, with the transitional-to-final gap narrowing as MAS's output floor steps from 60% in 2026 to 72.5% on 1 January 2029 ([OCBC 3Q26 press release](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2026/OCBC%203Q26%20Results%20Press%20Release.pdf); [Allen & Gledhill on the MAS circular](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/sg/publication/articles/24869/mas-issues-circular-on-implementation-timeline-for-the-final-basel-iii-reforms-in-singapore)). A new layer is coming: MAS's May 2026 consultation proposes external TLAC of 14% of RWA for local D-SIBs with a five-year phase-in and no counting of CCB-funded CET1, which arithmetically OCBC already covers but which will require eligible senior non-preferred-style issuance ([MAS Consultation Paper P011-2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/publications/consultations/rso/consultation-paper-on-proposed-tlac-framework-for-dsibs-in-singapore.pdf)). MAS's 2025 industry-wide stress test found that under an adverse scenario of a deep Singapore recession, 46–74% equity falls and 25–45% property falls, D-SIB aggregate CET1 would bottom at 9.8% in Year 2, above the 9.0% requirement, with each bank individually compliant ([MAS Financial Stability Review 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/publications/financial-stability-review/2025/financial-stability-review-2025.pdf)). OCBC is rated Aa1 by Moody's and AA- by S&P and Fitch; solvency is not the constraint on a new analytics budget, earnings sensitivity is.

### Market, rate and liquidity risk: a S$1.95bn supervisory NII shock, a bond-heavy insurer

Under the supervisory "Parallel Down" IRRBB scenario OCBC's one-year net interest income would fall by **S$1,951m** (2024: S$1,896m), higher because floating-rate exposure grew, while the "Parallel Up" scenario cuts economic value of equity by S$1,062m; the bank is therefore asset-sensitive to SGD rates and, with a lag, to USD rates, and management said in 2024 it had trimmed NII sensitivity to about S$4m per basis point using fixed-rate loans and cash-flow hedges (S$12.3bn of hedged assets at end-2025) ([AR2025, Note 38](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [The Edge Singapore, Aug 2024](https://www.theedgesingapore.com/news/banking-finance/ocbc-turns-defensive-protect-interest-income-rate-cuts-loom)). Trading risk is small (average aggregate VaR S$7.8m in 2025, five back-testing exceptions), funding is 80% customer deposits with CASA at 48.4%, USD loans-to-deposits of about 49% against 78.5% in SGD explain why rising US rates lift asset yields over time, and wholesale issuance spans a US$10bn covered-bond programme, AMTN, a US$1bn Tier 2 and the tokenised USCP programme ([FY25 results presentation](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20results%20presentation.pdf)). ### The exact MAS rules a third-party analytics or AI vendor must satisfy

A vendor to OCBC sits inside a layered regime, summarised below. The practical design consequences are: host in Singapore or at least under Singapore control (offshore processing of customer information triggers extra due diligence, foreign-supervisor-access policies and a 14-working-day MAS notification undertaking); accept a contract giving OCBC, MAS and MAS-appointed auditors audit and inspection rights, need-to-know access, no onward disclosure, breach notification, deletion on exit, prior notice of sub-contractors, notice before AI is introduced or changed, performance guarantees and exit portability; give OCBC "indirect control" over patching, MFA, encryption, API-key management, logging and penetration testing; supply enough model documentation, training-data provenance, evaluation results on OCBC's own data and prompt/output logs for OCBC to inventory, risk-rate and independently validate the use case; and accept that research or advice outputs reaching clients are a higher-materiality use case requiring human review before publication.

### Macro backdrop: an AI-led upcycle that MAS expects to cool through 2027

Singapore's economy grew 5.4% year on year in 3Q26 after 5.9% in 2Q and 6.3% in 1Q, MTI raised its 2026 forecast to 4.5–5.5% in August, and MAS held its S$NEER appreciation path on 14 October 2026 with core inflation seen at 1.5–2.0% in 2026 and 1.5–2.5% in 2027, warning that "global financial conditions are expected to tighten, and the AI-driven growth cycle is expected to ease" with AI-related activity "moderating in the latter half of 2027"; professional forecasters put 2027 growth at 3.1% ([MAS Monetary Policy Statement, 14 Oct 2026](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/monetary-policy-statements/2026/mas-monetary-policy-statement-14oct26); [MTI, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.mti.gov.sg/newsroom/mti-upgrades-2026-gdp-growth-forecast-to--4-5-to-5-5-per-cent-/)). The Fed held at 3.75–4.00% on 28 October 2026 with two dissents favouring a hike, so OCBC's "one more hike" assumption is consistent with the FOMC's own split ([Federal Reserve, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm)). Tariff risk has narrowed: the Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20 February 2026, a 10% Section 122 surcharge replaced them for Singapore and others, and Section 232 duties target semiconductors ([WilmerHale, 20 Feb 2026](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260220-supreme-court-strikes-down-ieepa-tariffs-what-now)). MAS's FSR flags stretched AI equity valuations, private-credit losses, hyperscalers' "novel and potentially circular private financing arrangements" and CRE as the system's key risks ([MAS FSR 2025](https://www.mas.gov.sg/-/media/mas-media-library/publications/financial-stability-review/2025/financial-stability-review-2025.pdf)). The 2025–2026 story was "NIM down, volumes and fees up"; the 2027 setup is the reverse, with a Fed on hold-to-hiking, SORA stabilised near 1.1–1.2% and a 14%-larger loan base supporting NII while fee income becomes more cyclical if AI-led exuberance cools. In order of likely P&L impact, the risks to model are a Middle East energy shock, an AI-capex pullback hitting TMT and digital-infrastructure lending and wealth flows, Indonesia, and Hong Kong and China CRE values.

## Where an AI forecasting and research-publishing platform fits, who sponsors it, and how to pitch it

### What the user already has, stated honestly

**BharatFinQA** ("Benchmarking Cross-Sectoral Financial Reasoning in the Indian Corporate Ecosystem") is an evidence-grounded financial-QA benchmark built from FY2025 annual reports of 187 NSE/BSE companies: 5,618 human-verified items (5,050 standard plus 568 adversarial unanswerable probes) across seven task families from extraction to multi-hop synthesis and sentiment–financial divergence, produced by an OCR-to-Markdown, section-aware chunking, relevance-scoring and persona-prompted generation pipeline that enforces "a 100% verbatim evidence copy-paste rule", and scored by a tier-aware protocol (BFQ-Score) with a pinned LLM judge ([BharatFinQA paper source](file:///Volumes/Elements/Abhay%27s%20MacBook%20M3%20Pro/BharatFinQA/BharatFinQA_Oct2026_OVERLEAF.tex); [pipeline README](file:///Volumes/Elements/Abhay%27s%20MacBook%20M3%20Pro/BharatFinQA/FinBharat-Report-QA-Benchmark/README.md)). Its headline result is that a US-benchmark-specialised model gains +31.7 points on the benchmark family it was trained on and "nothing measurable on Indian filings" (difference-in-differences +29.2, 95% CI +19.1 to +39.7), that the best generalist scores about 80–82% judged correctness, that judge-instruction changes move scores by 10.9 points against 3.5 for judge-model changes, and that human agreement is κ 0.711 on annotation and 0.742 on judge validation ([rebuttal README](file:///Volumes/Elements/Abhay%27s%20MacBook%20M3%20Pro/BharatFinQA/rebuttal_aug2026/README.md)). It was resubmitted to ACL ARR October 2026 as submission #4503 after a "3 = Findings" meta-review in the August cycle; it is under review, not accepted, and reviewers' open concerns (no expert-authored queries, no retrieval track, 41% majority-flag label noise on the Hard tier) should be disclosed rather than hidden ([session transcript](file:///Users/abhay/.claude/projects/-Users-abhay-BharatFinQA/bc4785a9-5d9e-4233-8cdd-c79f88e117fa.jsonl)).

**FinSafeQA-XL** is the more OCBC-relevant asset. The draft ("FinSafeQA: A Point-in-Time, Evidence-Grounded Benchmark for Safe-Asset Financial QA", targeted at ACL 2027 via the 4 January 2027 ARR deadline) describes **118,912 QA pairs across 13 markets and 114 instruments, "every item stamped with a source URL, document hash, and as-of date"**, a 3,160-item stratified evaluation split, a three-condition protocol (closed-book, BM25 retrieval, oracle evidence) and a 12-model sweep in which "closed-book accuracy never exceeds .211 ... while oracle accuracy caps at .728: the binding constraint is using context, not having it" ([FinSafeQA ACL draft](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/paper/acl/main.tex); [RESEARCH.md](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/RESEARCH.md)). The pool is dominated by machine-verified records from official APIs, including about 76,000 Singapore items built from MAS SGS bond, T-bill and SSB data, CPF rate histories (126 quarterly and 7,060 daily records with URL, SHA-256 and fetch-date provenance) and SDIC deposit-insurance rules, and the harvest exposed a real data-quality trap: legacy SORA answers keyed to publication date rather than value date ([RESEARCH.md](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/RESEARCH.md)). The intervention study yields bank-grade engineering findings: supervised fine-tuning "buys accuracy but destroys abstention (not-found recall .92→.51)", holdout-DPO "repairs abstention almost for free", correctness-conditioned confidence labels add 23 points of abstention at 1.5B parameters while cutting calibration error 57%, a calculator tool lifts a small model's financial-calculation accuracy from .094 to .306, and "no tuned variant beats its base under RAG or oracle" ([FinSafeQA README](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/README.md); [E24 tables](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/experiments/server/E24_tables.md)). Human-versus-judge agreement is still "TBD" with annotation due mid-November, the licence is "research preview — do not redistribute" pending review, and retrieval is BM25-only, so the honest framing is "in preparation for ACL 2027 with dataset, code and protocol built" ([project memory](file:///Users/abhay/.claude/projects/-Volumes-Elements-FinsafeQA/memory/finsafeqa-paper-project.md)). Two side studies transfer directly: a reproducible event study of nine NSE sector indices around 61 RBI policy announcements (market-model abnormal returns, Holm correction, bootstrap, panel fixed effects, 19 unit tests) and 322 instrument-years of safe-asset rate history with nine stress events ([RBI event study README](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/hypothesis/rbi-sector-stock-study/README.md)).

**morluto/rea** is "REA: Reverse Engineer Anything", an MIT-licensed TypeScript CLI and MCP server (version 6.8.0, created April 2026, very active with eight-plus contributors in the first week of November) for reverse-engineering binaries, Electron apps, .NET, APKs, firmware and websites; a case-insensitive search of the clone finds "ocbc" only inside a package-lock integrity hash and "singapore" nowhere, so it reveals nothing about OCBC and should not be cited as a source about the bank ([rea README](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Volumes-Elements-Abhay-s-MacBook-M3-Pro-OCBC-Bank/99364602-dc2c-414a-937c-1a49f8f37685/scratchpad/rea-repo/README.md); [GitHub](https://github.com/morluto/rea)). Its transferable ideas are architectural: an evidence contract that separates "observations, derivations, inferences, and unknowns" with provenance on every result, a layered domain-to-adapter architecture, MCP packaging so any agent can call analytics tools, and a "record a session, turn its API into tools, export an environment" pattern for testing agents over recorded internal traffic without production access ([rea AGENTS.md](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Volumes-Elements-Abhay-s-MacBook-M3-Pro-OCBC-Bank/99364602-dc2c-414a-937c-1a49f8f37685/scratchpad/rea-repo/AGENTS.md)). The 437,368-star count returned by the GitHub API is implausible for a seven-month-old project and should not be quoted. Two hygiene items precede any outreach: API keys and server credentials pasted into the BharatFinQA session should be rotated, and the user should confirm whether a different "rea" repository was intended.

### How to pitch it so leadership onboards or invests

First, speak OCBC's language and accept its premise that "AI is just a tool": present the platform as fit-for-purpose ADD, data first, deterministic guardrails second, models third, with a human in the loop before anything reaches a client, which mirrors both the WoW architecture and the AIRG's expectation of human review for financial-advisory use cases ([OCBC release, 1 Jul 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-unveils-avatar-banking)). Second, lead with measurement, because that is the gap every critic names and the CEO concedes: DBS reports about S$1bn of AI value and OCBC's 2027 target of "2x revenue contributed by AI" has no baseline, so the platform's first deliverable should be a forecast-accuracy and AI-value ledger that scores OCBC's own published calls (the 2026 Fed, GDP and gold revisions are the ready-made test set) and attributes revenue and cost outcomes to specific AI use cases ([AR2024](https://ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/creating-value-through-ai.page); [The Asian Banker, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/how-can-ocbc-sustain-its-financial-strength-amid-structural-change)). Third, demonstrate on what OCBC already talks about: an AI-equity valuation scenario dashboard that quantifies capex payback and hyperscaler debt paths behind Chez Anbu's "claim on future profits" argument, and a Singapore rates dashboard that tracks SORA, SOFR and HIBOR against management's FY26 assumptions, both published through a modern portal that fixes the stale research hub. Fourth, meet the infrastructure and cost constraints head on: deploy on OCBC's on-premise GPUs, Cloudera and OpenShift estate with open-weight models and frontier escalation, citing FinSafeQA's own evidence that open 7–8B models reach .65 oracle accuracy against .73 for the frontier, and that grounding in dated official documents beats fine-tuning, which keeps the cost line and the "very cost conscious" CEO on side ([E24 tables](file:///Volumes/Elements/FinsafeQA/experiments/server/E24_tables.md)). Fifth, arrive compliance-ready: a Notice 658-compliant contract (audit rights for OCBC and MAS, need-to-know access, no onward disclosure, deletion on exit, sub-contractor and AI-change notification, exit portability), Singapore hosting, FSM-N05/N06 controls, PDPA data-intermediary terms, FAA-G13 conflict controls on any published research, and an AIRG evidence pack that lets Carina Lee's team inventory, rate and validate the use case before the October 2027 deadline. Sixth, size it for OCBC's buying behaviour: the bank has no venture arm, buys through partnerships with regulated counterparties and prefers builds its engineers can extend, so propose a time-boxed pilot owned by the Group Data Office with one business sponsor (Global Markets or Bank of Singapore), procured under the CFO, with success defined in the same revenue and productivity terms OCBC already uses for its adviser-training programme ([OCBC release, 15 Apr 2026](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2026/ocbc-launches-generative-ai-powered-skills-training-for-all-wealth-advisors.page)). Seventh, time it: the next catalysts are MAS's FSR and MTI's 2027 forecast in late November 2026, FY2026 results in late February 2027 when the capital-return special is paid and 2027 guidance set, the Punggol hub opening in 1Q27, and the AIRG governance deadline in October 2027.

The objections are predictable. "We build in-house" is answered by shipping the pipeline, judges and verifier as components the Group Data Office can own, not a black box. "Costs are under scrutiny" is answered by on-premise deployment and a value ledger that reports its own payback. "Outages are a board topic" is answered by classifying the pilot outside OCBC's critical systems and keeping client channels for phase two. "The family is conservative" is answered by a pilot-sized commitment tied to measurable wealth-fee and research-productivity outcomes rather than a platform bet. And the credibility risk of overclaiming is answered by describing the papers exactly as they are: a benchmark under ACL review with a Findings-level prior meta-review, and an ACL 2027 draft whose Singapore corpus, protocol and training findings already exist.

## Conclusion

The research changes the shape of the opportunity. OCBC is not an AI laggard that needs capability; it is a bank with production agents, six million daily AI decisions and a billion-dollar technology budget that has chosen not to measure, model or publish, and whose research function has been consolidated under Global Markets with an explicit but unresourced mandate to become a multi-asset platform. The gap between what OCBC does and what it can show is widest exactly where a forecasting, research-publishing and dashboard platform operates: forecast tracking, valuation and scenario models, AI-value attribution and modern distribution of house views. MAS's October 2026 AI guidelines convert that gap into a dated obligation, since every AI use case must be inventoried, risk-rated and validated on the bank's own data by October 2027, and a vendor whose product generates that evidence as a by-product sells compliance and analytics at once.

The second shift is in how the user's own work should be presented. The Indian benchmark is not the asset; the discipline is, and FinSafeQA-XL's hash-stamped, as-of-dated Singapore safe-asset corpus plus its findings that grounding beats fine-tuning and that abstention must be diagnosed before it is trained are the exact arguments a conservative, scam-scarred, cost-conscious bank needs to hear before it lets an assistant speak to a Premier client. Pitched as a fit-for-purpose ADD component that Global Markets and the Group Data Office can own, that runs on-premise, that scores itself, and that arrives with its MAS paperwork written, the proposal lands on the one thing OCBC's 2026 outperformance has not bought it: proof.
