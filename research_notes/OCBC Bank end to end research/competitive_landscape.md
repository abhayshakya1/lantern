### Inferences
- DBS's AI narrative is quantified in a way OCBC's is not: a yearly economic-value number, model and use-case counts, prompt volumes and agent counts. Any vendor pitch to OCBC will be judged against the DBS disclosure template.
- DBS's wealth AUM (S$544bn) is roughly 1.5x OCBC's banking wealth AUM (S$358bn) and 2.7x UOB's (S$204bn) at Sep 2026, so wealth scale is a structural gap for OCBC rather than a cyclical one.

- DBS Group Research is positioning itself as a thought-leadership publisher on AI (two major AI reports in 2026), which raises the bar for OCBC's research visibility.
