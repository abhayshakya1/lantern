## 1. AI programmes: OCBC GPT, AI Lab, models and use cases in production, AI in wealth/credit/anti-fraud/service/coding, "AI-first" ambitions, agentic pilots, AI hiring, 2025 annual report and 2026 results commentary

### Takeaway
OCBC has run an in-house AI unit since 2018, rolled OCBC GPT (Azure OpenAI, inside Microsoft Teams) to ~30,000 staff in November 2023, reports "300+" AI use cases and ~6 million AI decisions a day (2024 annual report), and in 2026 under new CEO Tan Teck Long has made "AI, Digital and Data (ADD)" the core of its "Next Frontier" strategy, with a stated plan to spend more than S$1 billion a year on technology for three years, a July 2026 "avatar banking" app (OCBC WoW, in beta), and agentic AI in KYC/onboarding (Source of Wealth Assistant, HELIOS). The bank has not published an AI ROI framework; the CEO declined to say how returns will be measured.

### Cited Findings

**AI Lab / Group Data Office (history)**
- 14 March 2018 (period: 2018): OCBC launched "AI Lab@TOV" inside The Open Vault at OCBC fintech unit with an initial S$10 million over three years, headed by Ken Wong, starting with three data scientists and describing an "AI-first" strategy for digital capabilities; initial use cases were speech/NLP for local accents and facial recognition (AWS DeepLens) — [OCBC release](https://ocbc.com/group/media/release/2018/ocbc-launches-ai-lab)
- By September 2019 (period: 2019) the lab employed about 30 data scientists — [eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2019/09/ocbc-ai-lab); Ken Wong left in 2021 for Visa — [eFinancialCareers](https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2021/09/ken-wong-ocbc-to-visa)
- 8 June 2022 (period: 2022): Donald MacDonald appointed first Head of Group Data Office effective 1 June 2022 (acting since June 2021), reporting to Group COO Lim Khiang Tong; OCBC said the AI Lab is "one of the largest data science teams in Singapore" (no headcount) — [OCBC release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2022/ocbc-new-group-data-head.page)
- The 2024 annual report states the 2018 AI unit "has since become the Group Data Office", that "about six million decisions in the Bank are made by AI each day" and that the bank has "over 300 AI use cases" — [OCBC 2024 AR, Creating Value Through AI](https://ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2024-annual-report/creating-value-through-ai.page)

**OCBC GPT and generative AI tools**
- November 2023 (period: 2023): OCBC GPT, a ChatGPT-based chatbot, rolled out to ~30,000 employees globally after a six-month trial (April–September 2023) with ~1,000 staff in investment research, product management and marketing; participants self-reported completing tasks ~50% faster including time to fact-check output; OCBC Wingman coding assistant introduced May 2023 improved developer productivity ~20% — [OCBC release](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-is-first-singapore-bank-to-roll-out-generative-ai-chatbot-to-all-employees-globally.page)
- Microsoft's case study (8 November 2023): built in OCBC's private Azure environment with access to OpenAI models, embedded in Microsoft Teams; ~200 staff joined the pilot after a May 2023 event, ~1,000 regular users by September 2023 across Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Indonesia and Bank of Singapore; survey: 93% satisfied, 72% reported productivity gains; Wingman estimated 20–30% developer uplift; AML system previously generated >10,000 alerts a month at ~45 minutes each, now prioritised by an AI model; named executives Bryan Lee (Managing Director, Group Technology Architecture) and Donald MacDonald (Head of Group Data Office) — [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/features/ocbcs-new-generative-ai-chatbot-is-boosting-the-banks-productivity-across-departments-and-locations/)
- January 2024 interview: OCBC has used LLM technology since 2019 (BERT, FinBERT, then GPT-class); ~80% of work still traditional ML; GenAI focus areas are productivity, risk management and front-end revenue; "Buddy" internal chatbot navigates >150,000 pages of bank resources; Wingman v1 built in-house with open-source tools in under two weeks at near-zero cost, rolling to ~2,000 developers; GenAI runs largely on-premise on OCBC's "large GPU infrastructure" with "effectively zero" incremental cost; MacDonald said GenAI was not yet ready for direct customer exposure due to hallucination risk — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/ocbcs-donald-macdonald-genai-integration-increased-efficiency)
- March 2024: The Asian Banker gave OCBC "Best Productivity, Automation and Efficiency Initiative" for "5 GenAI use cases", citing 30,000 staff and "50% productivity gains" (the five use cases are not named on the page) — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/best-productivity-automation-and-efficiency-initiative-is-ocbc-for-its-5-genai-use-cases-2024)
- OCBC GPT had answered "over 1 million prompts as of May 2024" — reported by [OpenGov Asia, 17 June 2024](https://archive.opengovasia.com/2024/06/17/exclusive-driving-efficiency-and-innovation-ocbcs-journey-with-generative-ai/) (snippet only; the page refused automated fetch, so the figure is unverified against the full article)

### Inferences
- OCBC's AI narrative shifted in 2026 from internal productivity (OCBC GPT, Wingman, Buddy) to customer-facing wealth AI (avatars, advisor training, agentic onboarding); the "Whole-of-Wealth" positioning means wealth/private banking is the most receptive buying centre for analytics and research tools.
- Research publishing is already an AI target: HOLMES ingests 200,000 pages of research a day and OCBC WoW avatars are "trained on proprietary research-team data", so an external analytics/research-publishing platform would be positioned alongside in-house Group Data Office builds rather than into a vacuum.
- The absence of any published AI ROI methodology (CEO declined to define one, July 2026) and the 2027 "2x revenue from AI" target without a baseline suggest leadership is receptive to tools that quantify AI outcomes.
- Build-first culture: Wingman, Buddy, OCBC GPT, A.I. Oscar, HELIOS and the hackathon screening agents were all built in-house by the Group Data Office/engineering; vendors appear mainly as infrastructure (Azure OpenAI, Cloudera, Red Hat) rather than application providers.

- "Document AI" as a named internal tool appeared only in a search summary; not verified on any fetched page.

## 8. Cybersecurity and scams: December 2021 SMS phishing (losses, payouts, MAS capital penalty), later incidents and outages 2023–2026, anti-scam measures (kill switch, Money Lock, cooling-off, anti-malware) and results

### Takeaway
The December 2021 SMS-spoofing scam (790 victims, S$13.7 million fully reimbursed as a one-off goodwill payout) led MAS on 26 May 2022 to impose a 1.3x operational-risk RWA multiplier (~S$330 million extra capital) for deficient incident response, and it has shaped OCBC's posture since: kill switch (February 2022), 12-hour cooling-off periods, Android anti-malware blocking (August 2023, >S$2 million saved in a month), Money Lock (November 2023, >S$3.3 billion locked within two months), in-app calls (2025) and AI payment screening in under 15 ms; outages recurred (28 August 2023, 18 December 2025, 23 October 2026) and a May 2026 dark-web "breach" claim against OCBC Malaysia was found to be fabricated.

### Cited Findings
- Scam losses and payouts: reported losses S$13.7 million, mostly in December 2021; OCBC made goodwill payouts of the full amount to 790 affected customers, starting 8 January 2022 and completed by 30 January 2022 — [Yahoo/Reuters, 27 May 2022](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/mas-imposes-additional-s-330-m-capital-requirement-on-ocbc-over-sms-scams-033414171.html); [Malay Mail, 18 Jan 2022](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2022/01/18/ocbc-phishing-scam-goodwill-payouts-for-30-victims-to-date/2035840); MAS called the payouts a "one-off gesture" that "does not set a general precedent" — [Yahoo Malaysia](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/ocb-cs-goodwill-payouts-to-scam-victims-were-one-off-gesture-mas-says-084746408.html)
- MAS penalty (26 May 2022): additional capital requirement of ~S$330 million via a 1.3x multiplier on operational-risk RWA for OCBC's Singapore banking operations until gaps are addressed; reduced capital ratios by 0.21 percentage points; MAS's Marcus Lim: "Financial institutions have a duty to put in place robust measures to prevent, detect and respond to scams"; Helen Wong acknowledged OCBC "should have responded faster and better to early signs of the attacks" — [Yahoo/Reuters](https://sg.finance.yahoo.com/news/mas-imposes-additional-s-330-m-capital-requirement-on-ocbc-over-sms-scams-033414171.html); OCBC's statement: independent consultant found no cyberattack or system breach; remediation included a 24-hour (later 12-hour) cooling-off for digital-token provisioning, SMS Sender ID registry, removal of clickable links, lower default PayNow limits, a permanent fraud team, a 12-hour cooling-off for key account changes, the kill switch and staff seconded to the Police Anti-Scam Centre — [OCBC statement](https://ocbc.com/group/media/release/2022/media-statement-mas-response.page). ## 11. Gaps relative to peers: where analysts or press say OCBC trails DBS or UOB in technology or AI

### Takeaway
Press and analyst commentary consistently places DBS ahead on AI scale, disclosure and cost efficiency (430+ use cases, 2,000+ models, ~S$1 billion of stated AI value, 10 million customers on AI assistants, lowest CIR, ROE 16.2% vs OCBC 12.6%), while OCBC's AI claims are older (2024 counts), wealth-niche (avatar beta for 50 users), unmeasured (no ROI method) and its infrastructure is hybrid/on-prem rather than DBS's targeted "fully cloud-optimised stack"; UOB trails on efficiency (CIR 44.6%) but has broader staff-tool adoption (35,000 Copilot users).

### Cited Findings
- Use-case and value disclosure gap: DBS >430 use cases, >2,000 models, ~S$1 billion economic value (2025); OCBC's latest count "300+" from the 2024 report; UOB >300 incl. analytics; DBS Joy/digibot serve >10 million customers while OCBC WoW is in invitation-only beta — [Fintech News Singapore](https://fintechnews.sg/137393/ai/how-dbs-ocbc-and-uob-are-betting-on-ai-in-2026/); [DBS CIO statement](https://www.dbs.com/annualreports/2025/cio-statement.html)
