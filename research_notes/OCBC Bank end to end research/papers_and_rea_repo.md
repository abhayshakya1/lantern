### Inferences
- The paper is complete and under review, not accepted; the honest framing for OCBC is "a peer-reviewed-track benchmark currently at ACL ARR (Findings-level meta-review in the previous round)". Reviews from the October cycle are expected in December 2026.
- The most transferable asset is not the Indian dataset itself but the **pipeline + evaluation discipline**: OCR→Markdown→section-aware chunking→relevance scoring→persona-prompted QA generation with verbatim evidence, then a pinned LLM judge, human-validation protocol, and a verifier that recomputes every published number. All of that is corpus-agnostic and would run on SGX annual reports.
- The failure taxonomy (unit-scale errors such as lakh/crore vs 2-2-3 grouping, fiscal-year confusion, figures-without-inference lenience in judges) is a ready-made checklist for an OCBC document-QA risk review; Singapore analogues are S$ vs US$ scale, FY vs calendar year, basis points.

## Key question 4: Synthesis — reusable components and ideas mapped to OCBC's businesses; gaps and quality issues

### Takeaway
Across the three sources, what already exists and can be shown is: a corpus-agnostic **annual-report → evidence-grounded QA pipeline** (BharatFinQA) that would run on SGX filings; a **Singapore-ready, point-in-time, hash-pinned safe-asset knowledge base and abstention-aware evaluation protocol** (FinSafeQA) with MAS/CPF/SDIC data already harvested; **rate-transmission and policy-event-study tooling** (RBI event study, 20-year instrument histories) that transfers to MAS/SORA/STI; and a **publishing discipline** (pinned judges, verifier scripts that recompute every figure, datasheets, Croissant metadata, LaTeX/PDF/HTML artifact generation) that is the backbone of a research-publishing platform. The rea repo contributes only architectural patterns (evidence provenance, MCP tool packaging, recorded-API agent sandboxes).

### Inferences — "ideas to carry into an OCBC proposal", mapped to business lines

1. **Equities and securities research (OCBC Securities / research desk).** Re-run the BharatFinQA pipeline on SGX-listed annual reports and SGXNet announcements to build an evidence-grounded "ask the filing" assistant and an internal benchmark of which LLMs read Singapore filings reliably; keep the forensic-auditor question families (OCF-vs-PAT divergence, DuPont, related-party transactions, cross-table contradictions, governance risk, sentiment–financial divergence) as automated red-flag screens for research notes. Every answer ships with verbatim evidence and a judge-validated confidence, which is what compliance review needs.
2. **Wealth and retail advisory.** FinSafeQA already holds Singapore safe-asset content (CPF OA/SA/MA/RA, SSB, SGS, T-bills, SDIC) with as-of dates and document hashes; extend it with OCBC product terms (deposits, structured deposits, insurance) to power a point-in-time product-knowledge base. Use the three-condition protocol and calibration map as the pre-deployment gate for any customer-facing assistant, the "advice-boundary compliance" idea (MAS FAA constraints on personalised advice) as a scored behaviour, and the finding that grounding beats fine-tuning as the architecture decision.
3. **Treasury and markets / forecasting.** Port the RBI event-study framework to MAS policy statements and SORA/S$NEER moves against STI sector indices and OCBC's own rate-sensitive books; reuse the safe-asset dependency map to build a Singapore rate-transmission map (MAS policy → SORA → deposit/loan rates → SGS curve → CPF/SSB administered rates); reuse the 20-year instrument histories and stress-event catalogue as backtest scaffolding. (The drive also holds `Kronos-master.zip`, apparently a financial time-series model repo, not opened in this research.)
4. **Risk and model governance.** The judge-pinning, verifier-script, inter-annotator-agreement and re-verification machinery is a ready evidence pack for model-risk management: pinned prompts with SHA-256, blind judges, κ/AC1 reporting, flag-rate re-weighting, seed confidence intervals, and the explicit "every number recomputed from raw predictions" rule.
5. **Research publishing platform.** The two projects show the end-to-end loop already working: data harvest with provenance → experiments on a GPU farm → results-of-record markdown (RESULTS.md/E24_tables.md) → figure scripts → LaTeX/PDF/HTML artifact → datasheet/Croissant release. For OCBC this becomes: market data and filings → analytics and forecasts → auto-drafted research notes with evidence links and a verifier gate → publication to an internal portal, with a PIT corpus so every note is reproducible "as of" its date.
6. **Cost-aware model strategy.** FinSafeQA's idea-01 (small models plus the right harness approach frontier quality; FrugalGPT/RouteLLM cascades) and the measured sweep (open 8B models reach .65 oracle vs .73 frontier) support a pitch for routing most workloads to small on-prem models with frontier escalation.
7. **Agent sandboxing (from rea).** Build agent test environments from recorded internal API traffic so analytics agents can be evaluated without production access, with evidence-gated writes.
