# OCBC Group's in-house research, forecasting and market-view output (as of 7 November 2026)

Scope note: this covers every research/forecasting function across OCBC Group (OCBC Group Research / Global Markets, OCBC Wealth Panel and Wealth Advisory, Bank of Singapore CIO office, OCBC Securities / OCBC Investment Research, Lion Global Investors, Great Eastern), what each publishes, how it is distributed, current house views (H2 2026), AI/data tooling, gaps versus DBS/UOB, and forecaster awards. All figures in Singapore dollars are labelled S$; US-dollar figures are labelled US$. Every dated view is labelled with its date. Research method constraint: the session's web-search budget ran out after ~45 searches, so the final sweep relied on direct fetches of known URLs; several items (Bloomberg video, The Edge Singapore, Business Today Malaysia) were blocked (403/bot-wall) and are recorded as gaps.

## 1. OCBC Global Markets Research (now branded "OCBC Group Research") — leadership, coverage, publications, distribution, media

### Takeaway
OCBC's sell-side-style macro/FX/rates/credit/equity research is one team, branded "OCBC Group Research" (publications carry "By Global Markets" headers and the older "Global Markets Research & Strategy" / "Treasury Research" labels still appear), led by Chief Economist Selena Ling. It publishes a daily treasury note, weekly macro and FX notes, a monthly Research Monitor (the full forecast table), semi-annual Global Economic Outlook and FX & Rates Outlook decks, daily/weekly/monthly Asian credit notes, and a thin stream of Singapore equity notes; distribution is PDF on ocbc.com, email, Telegram ("OCBC Research Insights" podcasts), syndication to FXStreet/Bernama/The Edge, and TV (Bloomberg, CNA).

### Inferences
- The team is small relative to the product range: only ~9 named front-line names across macro, ASEAN, Greater China, FX/rates, commodities and credit were traceable in 2026 sources; analysts on daily credit and treasury notes are unnamed, which suggests production is templated and could be automated further.
- The 2025–26 rebrand from "Global Markets Research & Strategy"/"Treasury Research" to "OCBC Group Research" (and the absorption of the securities units into Global Markets) points to Global Markets (Kenneth Lai) as the natural executive sponsor for any platform touching macro, FX, rates, credit and equity research together.
- The public research hub is visibly stale (2004–2021 copyright, empty equity filter), so a vendor pitch on research-publishing infrastructure has a concrete, observable pain point.

### Gaps
- No complete 2026 team roster with titles was found (the Dec 2025 deck's last page is a disclaimer, not a roster; OCBC publishes no team page). Credit research author names (historically Andrew Wong, Ezien Hoo, Wong Hong Wei) could not be verified for 2026.
- The content of the "OCBC Global Economic Outlook 2H 2026 – The new normal…" (19 Jun 2026) and the monthly "Research Monitor (Oct 2026)" could not be retrieved (no PDF links exposed; search budget exhausted).
- The exact publication cadence of the Weekly Macro View vs FX Weekly (both appear roughly weekly but with gaps in listings) is not documented by OCBC.

## 2. OCBC Wealth Management research — the Wealth Panel, outlooks, Wealth Insights, Wealth Advisory, house-view process, model portfolios, app delivery

### Takeaway
The OCBC Wealth Panel (launched 2011) is a cross-entity committee of eight named senior people from OCBC Group Research, Bank of Singapore, Wealth Management and Lion Global Investors that produces a unified monthly house view (Global Outlook, Equities, Bonds, FX & Commodities, Wealth Insights) for Premier/Premier Private Client customers; the global equity and bond views are in practice written by Bank of Singapore's Eli Lee, macro by Selena Ling, FX/gold by Christopher Wong. Distribution is via ocbc.com Market Insights, the OCBC mobile app (daily updates and thematic ideas), emailed updates, monthly client sessions, Money Monday, webinars/videos, and since July 2026 the AI "avatar" app OCBC WoW (beta). A separate Wealth Advisory unit (Chez Anbu, Head of Wealth Advisory) writes OCBC's most explicit AI-valuation commentary as op-eds in The Edge Singapore.
