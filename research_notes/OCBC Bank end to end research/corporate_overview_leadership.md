## 1. History and the Lee family's role

### Takeaway
OCBC is Singapore's longest-established bank, formed in 1932 by merging three local banks (oldest founded 1912), and has grown mainly by acquisition (Keppel Capital 2001, Bank NISP 2004–05, ING Asia Private Bank 2010, Wing Hang 2014, Barclays Asia wealth 2016, NAB Asia private wealth 2017, Great Eastern stake build-up to 93.72% in 2024, Bank Commonwealth Indonesia 2024). The founding Lee family (Lee Kong Chian's descendants) remains the largest shareholder (~28% per Bloomberg, Feb 2026) with one family director, Prof Lee Tih Shih, who chairs the board's Executive Committee.

### Inferences
- The family's influence operates through (a) Selat/Lee Foundation/Lee Rubber-linked holdings (see Section 3), (b) Lee Tih Shih's Exco chairmanship, and (c) informal approval of major capital decisions. A vendor pitch should assume capital-intensive proposals face a conservative shareholder.

## 2. Group structure today and the status of the Great Eastern privatisation

### Takeaway
OCBC Ltd owns 100% of its banks in Malaysia (incl. OCBC Al-Amin), Hong Kong, Macau and China, 100% of Bank of Singapore and OCBC Securities, ~85% of OCBC Indonesia, ~96% of Lion Global Investors and 93.72% (economic) / ~88.2% (voting) of Great Eastern Holdings, plus 20% of Bank of Ningbo. The 2024–25 attempt to take Great Eastern private failed (delisting vote 8 Jul 2025 got 63.49% vs 75% needed); a 1-for-1 bonus issue restored the free float and trading resumed 21 Aug 2025; OCBC has said no further offer "in the foreseeable future" and CEO Tan Teck Long called it "a chapter behind us" on 25 Feb 2026. No 2026 offer has been made; GEH remains SGX-listed.
