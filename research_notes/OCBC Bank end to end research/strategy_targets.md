## Key Question 1: The 2022 corporate strategy refresh, the S$3 billion incremental revenue target and progress FY2022–FY2025

### Takeaway
OCBC's 2022 refresh was an eight-pillar, three-year plan (four "Drive Growth" priorities plus four "Reinforce Strengths" enablers, with "One Group" as a pillar); in July 2023 it was sharpened into an ASEAN–Greater China thesis with a target of S$3 billion cumulative incremental revenue over 2023–2025 "on top of current growth trajectory". OCBC says the S$3 billion target was surpassed in September 2025, ahead of time, and the 2022 strategy was formally closed with the launch of a successor strategy, "The Next Frontier", on 25 February 2026.

### Cited Findings

Design of the 2022 refresh
- Helen Wong's remarks at the February 2022 AGM describe a "3-year strategy refresh to drive growth and reinforce core strengths (2022–2024)" — [OCBC Group CEO remarks, 23 Feb 2022 (AGM slides)](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2021/presentation%20slides%20tabled%20at%202022%20agm.pdf) (seen via search snippet; PDF not opened).
- The 2022 Annual Report frames the refresh as "Drive Growth" and "Reinforce Strengths", with four growth priorities: (1) capture rising Asian wealth with Singapore–Hong Kong hubs and digital propositions; (2) support increasing ASEAN–Greater China trade and investment flows; (3) unlock value from the New Economy and high-growth industries; (4) drive the transition to a sustainable, low-carbon world — [OCBC AR2022, "Excel for sustainable growth"](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2022-annual-report/excel-for-sustainable-growth.page).
- The July 2023 investor deck (slide 6) lists all eight pillars: the four growth priorities above plus (5) forge a "One Group" integrated customer experience approach; (6) invest in accelerating transformation and digitalisation; (7) strengthen people assets and culture; (8) build on capital and risk management strengths — [OCBC ASEAN–Greater China Update, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf).
- Helen Wong, 3 July 2023: the unified brand "solidifies our One Group approach, one of eight core pillars of our Corporate Strategy that was refreshed in 2022" — [OCBC media release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth).
- Stated aspiration (2023 deck, slide 6): to be the "Leading Financial Services Partner for a Sustainable Asia"; wholesale aspiration: "Core Bank for our ASEAN customers in our core markets… and Regional Bank of Choice for Chinese Corporates venturing into ASEAN" — [3 Jul 2023 deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf).

The ASEAN–Greater China corridor thesis (as stated July 2023)
- ASEAN and Greater China's share of world GDP (PPP) rose from 23% (2013) to 26% (2022) with 27% forecast for 2025; ASEAN–China trade flows grew at a 13% CAGR from US$588 billion (2018) to US$975 billion (2022) — [3 Jul 2023 deck, slide 11](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf).
- OCBC total income almost doubled from S$6.7 billion (2013) to S$12.7 billion (2022); ASEAN contributed 75% of 2022 group income; Greater China's contribution more than tripled from 6% to 20%; Greater China income grew 6x (22% CAGR 2013–2022); OCBC held S$93 billion of total assets in Greater China plus a 20% stake in Bank of Ningbo; ~4,500 staff across more than 60 branches in 17 cities — [3 Jul 2023 deck, slides 9, 12, 13](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf).
- Helen Wong (3 Jul 2023): "The flow business between ASEAN and Greater China is not new to us"; drivers cited were China's post-pandemic reopening, ASEAN's rise under "China plus one" and other geopolitical factors — [OCBC release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth).
- Helen Wong (AR2024, published March 2025): "what we see is an evolving China plus one strategy into a China plus n strategy"; "In 2024, we saw an almost 30% year-on-year increase in new-to-bank customers which are Chinese companies coming to ASEAN"; Bank of Ningbo has ~480 branches/offices in China — [OCBC AR2024, Review by Group CEO](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2024/ocbc-annual-report-2024.pdf).

The S$3 billion target: definition and supporting goals
- Slide 14 (3 Jul 2023): "Our refreshed strategy aims to deliver an incremental S$3b revenue over the next 3 years… cumulative in 3 years (2023-25)… From ASEAN-Greater China focus, on top of current growth trajectory", with four accompanying goals: (1) anchor market leadership as Top 2 financial institution in ASEAN by assets; (2) strengthen income-generation capabilities structurally; (3) better operating efficiency and cost-income ratio; (4) focused capital deployment in high-growth ASEAN–Greater China markets — [3 Jul 2023 deck](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf).
- Helen Wong (3 Jul 2023): "We are therefore confident that we will be able to deliver S$3 billion in incremental revenue by 2025" — [OCBC release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth).
- Sub-targets announced the same day: more than S$50 million investment in Greater China transaction banking over three years; more than 500 regional cash-management mandates over five years; double investment-banking revenue in three years; more than 26,000 new SME customers in Hong Kong over three years; more than 50% increase in Greater China franchise revenue in ASEAN by 2025; S$140 million already invested in wholesale digital capabilities over the prior three years; corporate and commercial bankers to grow 30% to about 400 by 2024 (a 2021 projection reaffirmed) — [3 Jul 2023 deck, slides 18, 20, 21](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/ocbc-asean-greater-china-update.pdf); [OCBC release, 3 Jul 2023](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2023/ocbc-unifies-brand-solidifying-one-group-strategy-to-accelerate-asean-greater-china-growth).

### Inferences
- The S$3 billion target was a cumulative 2023–2025 figure measured against a 2023 baseline trajectory, not an annual run-rate; OCBC has never published the annual attribution beyond the S$500 million 2023 figure, so an external analyst cannot reconstruct how the S$3 billion was counted.
- The "Top 2 FI in ASEAN by assets" goal was effectively declared met: the Chairman's 2025 statement says the combined asset base of S$676 billion makes OCBC "the second largest financial services group in Southeast Asia" (see Question 2).
- The 2022–2025 language ("One Group", "twin hub", "wealth continuum", "follow the flow", "China plus n") carries straight into the 2026 strategy; a vendor pitch should use these phrases.

### Gaps
- No OCBC document found that states the FY2024 cumulative incremental revenue figure or the final total at September 2025; only "surpassed" is stated.
- The February 2022 AGM slide deck and the 2023 annual report were not opened directly; the 2022–2024 horizon is from a search snippet.
- The Edge Singapore results articles (FY2025 and others) returned HTTP 403 and could not be read.

## Key Question 2: New strategy, targets and organisational changes in 2026 under Group CEO Tan Teck Long ("The Next Frontier")

### Takeaway
Tan Teck Long became Group CEO on 1 January 2026 and launched "The Next Frontier" on 25 February 2026: four strategic shifts (Asia, Tech, Net-Zero, Franchise) with "ADD" (AI, Digital, Data) "at our core", a Singapore–Hong Kong twin-hub wealth model, and only soft financial targets ("stable to improving ROE", CIR "low to mid 40%"); supporting commitments disclosed later in 2026 include >S$1 billion a year of technology spend for three years, 600 new relationship managers over three years, and double-digit wealth AUM growth. No separate investor day was held; the strategy was presented with FY2025 results and restated at the 16 April 2026 AGM.

### Cited Findings

Leadership transition and mandate
- OCBC announced on 11 July 2025 that Tan Teck Long (Head of Global Wholesale Banking since March 2022; previously Group Chief Risk Officer at DBS) would become Group CEO on 1 January 2026, with Deputy CEO status immediately; Helen Wong retired 31 December 2025 and remains Chairman of OCBC China and a director of OCBC Hong Kong — [OCBC release, 11 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-appoints-tan-teck-long-as-group-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-helen-wong).
- Chairman Andrew Lee (11 Jul 2025): "there will be no disruption to OCBC's corporate strategic direction" of "becoming an integrated financial services powerhouse"; Tan will "refine the corporate strategy and build on the One Group approach" — [OCBC release, 11 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-appoints-tan-teck-long-as-group-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-helen-wong); [Fortune, 14 Jul 2025](https://fortune.com/asia/2025/07/14/ocbc-new-ceo-tan-teck-long-replaces-helen-wong-china-asean).
- Tan Teck Long (11 Jul 2025): "Helen has laid down a firm foundation and I will be privileged to build on that"; the group will "double down on pursuing strong sustainable growth, innovation and people development" — [OCBC release, 11 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-appoints-tan-teck-long-as-group-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-helen-wong).
- Tan has chaired the OCBC "Strategic Resilience Group" since May 2025 and sits on the boards of Bank of Ningbo and Maxwealth Fund Management — [OCBC release, 11 Jul 2025](https://www.ocbc.com/group/media/release/2025/ocbc-appoints-tan-teck-long-as-group-chief-executive-officer-succeeding-helen-wong).

The Next Frontier: content (25 Feb 2026 deck and AR2025)
- Megatrends named: heightened geopolitical tensions, shifting demographics, rising Asian wealth, AI and digitalisation, sustainability. Four strategic shifts with "ADD at our core" (Artificial Intelligence, Digital, Data) — [Group CEO deck, 25 Feb 2026, slide 5](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf).
- Asia Shift: "Tap rising Asia trade, investment and wealth flows"; "Capture ASEAN–Greater China flows, leveraging OCBC's regional network"; "'One-ASEAN' value proposition"; "Capture rising Asian wealth flows through our SG-HK twin wealth hubs approach" — [25 Feb 2026 deck, slide 6](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf).
- Tech Shift: "Embed customer centricity powered by ADD (AI, Digital, Data) and grow tech sectors"; "Customer Centric ADD Strategy – Right Customer, Right Product, Right Time"; "Intensify focus and coverage on technology, including digital infrastructure and tech supply chain" — [25 Feb 2026 deck, slide 7](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf).
- Net-Zero Shift: "Expand coverage of green sectors such as renewables and sectors transitioning to Net-Zero"; "Deepen support for green transition of SMEs in core markets" — [25 Feb 2026 deck, slide 8](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf).
- Franchise Shift: Twin Hubs — Singapore: "Deliver Whole-of-Wealth value proposition across Banking, Wealth and Insurance", "Lead in Silver proposition for the super-aged society"; Hong Kong: "Grow affluent segment in Hong Kong", "Scale up Global Markets in Hong Kong". ASEAN domestic markets — Malaysia: "Expand our affluent customer base by leveraging Great Eastern Malaysia ecosystem", "Capture inbound investment flows and leverage special economic zone (e.g., Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone)"; Indonesia: "Grow OCBC Private Bank in Indonesia" — [25 Feb 2026 deck, slide 9](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/quarterly-results/2025/ocbc%20fy25%20ceo%20presentation.pdf).

- CEO letter (AR2025, Feb 2026) on the four shifts: "an Asia shift, where we deepen our focus on Asia as a growth engine and strengthen connectivity within the region and beyond"; "a Tech shift, where we drive revenue through customer centricity enabled by AI, Digital and Data ('ADD') capabilities, and capture opportunities arising from digital infrastructure builds"; "a Net-Zero shift, where we support the transition to a low-carbon economy while managing climate-related risks responsibly"; "a Franchise shift, where we sharpen our positioning and scale in core markets and customer segments"; "OCBC enters The Next Frontier from a position of strength" — [OCBC AR2025 PDF](https://www.ocbc.com/iwov-resources/sg/ocbc/gbc/pdf/investors/annual-reports/2025/2025-annual-report-en.pdf); [AR2025 Group CEO's perspectives](https://www.ocbc.com/group/investors/annual-reports/2025-annual-report/group-ceos-perspectives.page).

## Key Question 9: What leadership says about forecasting, data, analytics, research and AI as strategic enablers
